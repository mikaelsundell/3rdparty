[LICENSE](LICENSE) covers this repository’s build scripts, not the dependencies.

The files below are verbatim copies from the extracted sources used by the local
build. Copyright years and authors are preserved as supplied upstream. They are
not replaced with the current year. The [manifest](licenses/manifest.json) records
each copied file’s source path and SHA-256 checksum.

and asset notices; retain those from the source distributions when packaging.

## Updating notices

When changing a dependency version, copy its notice files from that exact source
archive, preserve their contents, and update this index and the manifest. Do not
infer a new copyright range from the package version or from the current date.
